# Relational Ontology

ALMAS avoids single-label classification. A complete analysis may compare AF/KA/AG/LG structurally while also assigning states across independent relational axes.

## States

`SUPPORTED`, `COMPATIBLE`, `INSUFFICIENT`, `CONTRADICTED`, `NOT_EVALUABLE`.

## Axes

- **Origin:** independent, family/group, shared origin, monadic, split-soul, twin-flame model, indeterminate.
- **History:** new connection, familiarity, past-life interpretation, karmic debt/balance, contract/agreement, indeterminate.
- **Function:** companionship, learning, mirror, catalysis, healing, initiation, evolution, integration/hieros gamos, mission/service.
- **Polarity:** similarity, complementarity, mirror, erotic, archetypal.
- **Modality:** 3D/material, transitional, mixed 3D/5D, predominantly transpersonal.
- **Phase:** recognition, activation, crisis/mirror, separation, surrender, integration, reunion, service, closure, indeterminate.
- **Real viability:** unknown, stable, unstable, separated, non-romantic, no-contact, defined by facts.
- **Reciprocity:** bilateral, partial, asymmetric, not evaluable.

No axis automatically determines another. In particular, symbolic intensity does not determine real-world viability or reciprocity.
